"I watched the streaks of crimson red blood dance out in front of my eyes, and splatter into my limp hands."
3
&
This passage uses symbolic description. The reason this passage aligns with the fear emotion rather than the sad emotion is because the release of blood is a symbol for pain, and pain is usually used as a symbol for fear. For example, in a novel, if the character gets shot but survives, the pain emphasized by the bullet wound is usually paired with fear, unless the focus of the novel is NOT on the bullet. A symbol for sadness would be death, where all the activity and life is sucked away into a cold nothing, If someone is shot dead, fear comes first from the symbol of blood, then sadness comes after from the symbol of death.
